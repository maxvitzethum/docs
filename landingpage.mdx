# Main Menu

The **Main Menu** serves as the central entry point of the application. From here you can access the three main modules, each covering a specific aspect of process management and optimization.

## Process Manager

The **Process Manager** is the central hub for documenting and maintaining business processes.

Typical use cases include:

- Creating new process documentation
- Editing existing processes
- Managing process versions
- Maintaining process structures and metadata

<Info>
  Use the Process Manager whenever you want to document or update business
  processes.
</Info>

---

## Process Analysis

The **Process Analysis** module provides insights into your software landscape and helps identify optimization opportunities.

Features include:

- Software landscape analysis
- Process dependency analysis
- Identification of optimization potential
- Evaluation of existing process flows

<Tip>
  Regular analyses help identify inefficiencies and support continuous
  improvement.
</Tip>

---

## Potential Scout

The **Potential Scout** helps identify opportunities for increasing efficiency across your organization.

Typical capabilities include:

- Detection of optimization opportunities
- Identification of automation candidates
- Recognition of redundant activities
- Support for process improvement initiatives

<Note>
  Potential Scout complements the Process Analysis module by focusing on
  actionable improvement opportunities.
</Note>

---

## Navigation

To open a module:

1. Navigate to the **Main Menu**.
2. Click on the corresponding tile.
3. The selected module opens in a new workspace.
